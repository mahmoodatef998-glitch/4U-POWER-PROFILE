# Lead tracking — guide for the sales team

## WhatsApp reference codes
Every WhatsApp message sent from the website ends with a line like:

    Ref: 4U-7K2PX

1. Open **www.4ugenerators.com/admin/whatsapp** (admin user + password).
2. Search the code → you see the page the customer was on (e.g. `/en/generators/500-kva`), the button they pressed, and the channel (Google Ads, Google organic, Facebook…).
3. As the deal moves, set the status: `contacted` → `quoted` → `won` / `lost`, and enter the **deal value in AED** when won.
4. The cards at the top show clicks, quotes, deals won and revenue for the last 30 days, plus the top pages and channels.

If a customer deletes the code, ask which page they were looking at — or just mark the chat in your notes.

## Website forms (quote, contact, calculator, datasheet, cart, spare parts)
All appear on **/admin** with their source and campaign. Spare-parts requests show `parts` as the source with links to the uploaded photos (links are valid for 1 hour after opening the page — refresh to get new ones). Update status and deal value the same way.

## Why it matters
Status `won` + deal value per lead is what lets us measure which pages, keywords and ad campaigns actually produce sales — and later feed them back to Google Ads so it optimises for buyers, not clicks.
